🗑️ Various Types of Waste Bins

🟢 Green Bin

Biodegradable / Wet Waste

Food waste, vegetable peels, fruit peels, leaves etc.

🔵 Blue Bin

Dry / Recyclable Waste

Paper, cardboard, plastic bottles, metal cans etc.

🟡 Yellow Bin

Special Waste

Batteries, medicines and certain chemical or medical waste.

⚫ Black Bin

General / Non-Recyclable Waste

Diapers, used tissues, thermocol and other general waste.
